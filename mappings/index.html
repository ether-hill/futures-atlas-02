Mappings — one evidence site, many datasets

Mappings borrows the anatomy of Mapping Police Violence — a headline figure, a map of incidents, trend and breakdown charts, and a filterable record table — and makes the data domain a variable.

Each mapping is a dataset module: openly licensed records with locations, dates and dimensions, plus its own methodology notes. The same widgets render whichever module is loaded.

Current mappings chart the AI compute buildout and AI investment, every record linking back to its source.
