Magnifica — one encyclical, many faiths

In May 2026 Pope Leo XIV published Magnifica Humanitas, the first papal encyclical on artificial intelligence. This experience lets you explore its structure and arguments, then asks a speculative question: what would the equivalent document look like from the world's other great faith traditions?

Sixteen leaders — from the Dalai Lama to the Grand Imam of Al-Azhar — are each given a predicted document: a title, a summary, and excerpts written in their voice, grounded in their real public statements on technology and clearly labeled as speculative.
