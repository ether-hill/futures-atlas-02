## Caption (≤150 words)
RSA is one of the main ways data is encrypted online, and a big enough quantum computer could break it.

Every year the Global Risk Institute and evolutionQ ask quantum experts the same question: how likely is a machine that can factor a 2048-bit number in under 24 hours within 5, 10, 15, 20 and 30 years?

In 2025, 26 experts answered. On the 10-year question, 13 called it unlikely (under 30%). 7 said about even odds and 6 said likely or more. Over 30 years, 22 of 26 called it likely.

The panel's 10-year odds are higher than in 2022: 10 to 27% then, 28 to 49% in 2025. Each survey counts 10 years from its own date. But the panel shrank from 40 to 26 and changes each year. These are informed opinions from people in the field, not a forecast with a track record.
