Levels

Levels. Standing waves in a box, one hump per rung, summed with the measured weights: the line is the state, the glow its density. The ladder is lit by what is ringing.

Orbit

Orbit. Bohr's rule: a whole number of wavelengths around a ring. Each ringing overtone is an n-lobed wave on its own shell; the ink line is the sum.

Arrows

Arrows. Feynman's arrows from QED: sixteen hands, one per overtone, turning n times as fast as the first, added tip to tail. The tip traces the voice's waveform.

Bars

Bars. Overtones 1 to 16, relative strength, strongest marked.
