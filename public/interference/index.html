Wave optics, running live

Interference studies

Eleven fields, each a different way of adding waves together, from two drips in a sink to the moment a fringe pattern stops existing. Every one is a single fragment shader over the pixels, computed fresh each frame, so nothing here is a video. Slow them down, re-colour them, open one full width, take a still or record a clip, or embed any of them somewhere else.

Palette

Colour is doing work here, not decoration. Where a field shows intensity, the darkest tone is zero and the lightest is the maximum for that field. Where it shows phase, hue runs once around the cycle and brightness falls to nothing wherever the phase has no single value, which is why the vortex cores read as black pinpricks rather than as gaps in the drawing.

Physics is simulated, not measured: these are the standard textbook fields evaluated per pixel, tuned for legibility rather than for a particular apparatus. Wavelengths and source spacings are chosen so the structure reads at this size. Where a panel names a real experiment, the experiment is real and the picture is an illustration of it.
