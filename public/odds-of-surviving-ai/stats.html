Every roll, spin and card pick since this went up, counted as it happened. It is a straight count of what happened, with nothing projected or sampled. Nothing about who played is stored: the page keeps a tally, and the tally keeps no names.

Nobody is asked to keep going. The odds are, by design, survivable: roughly three plays in four come out fine. Doom is something you have to keep playing for, and the number above is how many people did. Build the thing, see it go well, then build it again. That is roughly how AI policy works right now, which is why this number sits at the top of the page.
