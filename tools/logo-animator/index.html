Sphere & render

The numbers come from fa.svg itself. Along the diagonal the artwork is unbroken ink from 0.222 to 0.998 of the radius, which is one line running the full diameter; square to it there are three bands, at 0.224–0.396, 0.469–0.639 and 0.831–1.0. A ring tilted 51° about that diagonal projects to exactly those figures, so the crescents in the flat mark are one circle seen at an angle. Every part is round stock rather than flat: a tube of diameter 0.170, which is the same gauge as the sphere's own ring, swept round a circle of radius 0.875. Fitted that way it lands within 1.3% of the original, better than a flat ring manages, and nothing ever thins to nothing when it turns edge on.
